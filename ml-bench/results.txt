== 2026-06-19: QACC SIMD depthwise (ee.vmulas.s8.qacc) ==

Replaced scalar depthwise conv with SIMD using ESP32-S3 QACC accumulator
(16 independent 20-bit lanes). Vectorizes across channels instead of kernel
taps: each ee.vmulas.s8.qacc processes 16 channels in parallel for one tap.
Filter layout changed from [C,K] to [K,C] for contiguous 16-ch loads.
Input pre-padded to eliminate inner-loop bounds checks.

Self-test: all 4 depthwise configs (t=32/64/128/256, c=16/32) bit-exact
